- Devices & Components:
~ Arduino Nano Every
~ Femail jack
~ 4GB or larger Micro SD Card
~ AZ_Delivery 0.91 OLED I2C Display
~ MP3 TFT16 Player
~ Jumperwires
~ PS2 joystiuck for Arduino

- Project description:
This MP3 is just a protoype and is based on a female 3 pins jack that could be connected in this way: sleeve to ground, tip and ring respectively to speaker 1 and 2 on the 16P.
By the software side, songs can be skipped, paused and the volume can be regulated (all of this can be visualized by the OLED) by the joystick.
In order to insert songs, just put them in micro SD
-->(I numbered it with "01)
